OUR STORY

Built by an angler.

Being the son of an Air Force veteran, Kyle started his fishing adventure at 15. After his second trip in a 14’ Jon Boat, he landed his first 5lb’er and was instantly hooked. That passion became Bait Odyssey Custom Baits.

Today the focus is simple: quality baits at an honest price, with custom colors available by request.
